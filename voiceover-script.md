## 📋 RECORDING TIPS

1. **Use a good mic** - Built-in is fine, USB is better
2. **Record in a quiet room** - No background noise
3. **Speak clearly** - Pause between sentences
4. **Stand up** - Better voice projection
5. **Drink water** - Stay hydrated
