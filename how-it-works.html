⚡ What Is This Kit?

The Faceless Creator Kit is a complete template system for YouTube creators who don't show their face. It gives you premium, branded templates that work in Canva and Affinity Designer.

🎯 No design experience needed. Just open, edit, export, post.

🚀 How It Works

Here's the entire workflow in 4 simple steps:

1
Pick Your Tool

Choose Canva (free, web-based) or Affinity Designer (pro, one-time).

2
Open a Template Guide

Go to /templates/canva/ or /templates/affinity/ and pick your template.

3
Follow the Guide

Each guide has step-by-step instructions with exact settings.

4
Export & Post

Export as PNG, MP4, or SVG. Upload to YouTube, Instagram, or anywhere.

⏱️ Total time: 5–10 minutes per template.
